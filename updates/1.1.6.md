# What has been updated?

## Changelog - (Added, Fixed, Removed):

- Added office position(s) to buy warehouses through an office instead of buying directly on the warehouse storage location, the office also has a management section to manage purchased warehouses.
- **Removed** `PromptFooterRentingDisplay, PromptFooterDisplay, PurchaseDescription, Purchased` options on all `Config.Locations`, those displays are no longer required because of the NUI.
