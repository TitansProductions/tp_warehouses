# What has been updated?

## Changelog - (Added, Fixed, Removed):

- **Added** office position(s) to buy warehouses through an office instead of buying directly on the warehouse storage location, the office also has a management section to manage purchased warehouses.
- **Fixed** an issue on open storage key, even if it was not visible, players could still press (hold) and attempt to open main storage (it would throw an error or do no action). 
- **Removed** `PromptFooterRentingDisplay, PromptFooterDisplay, PurchaseDescription, Purchased` options on all `Config.Locations`, those displays are no longer required because of the NUI.

## Modified Files:

- client/nui (**add new folder with its contents**)
- client/tp-client_main.lua
- client/tp-client_escrow_ignore.lua (**add new file**)
- client/tp-client_functions.lua
- server/tp-server_callbacks.lua
- fxmanifest.lua

## Notes

- Replace on `Config.Locations` (all of them), where `RentingCost` to `TaxCost`.

- Modified `Config.PromptsKeys` where `WAREHOUSE` option is, replace `WAREHOUSE` to the new (it contains label).

Config.PromptsKeys = {
